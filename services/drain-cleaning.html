A clog is not a leak, but it is just as common a call

Nothing is escaping the pipe in a clogged drain — something is blocking it — but it is one of the most frequent calls we get in La Puente, often right alongside a leak inspection. Kitchen drains clog with grease that hard San Gabriel Basin water keeps from breaking down the way it would elsewhere. Bathroom drains clog with hair and soap scum that binds with mineral deposits into a stubborn mass. Older cast iron sewer laterals under La Puente's 1950s and 1960s tract blocks narrow with decades of scale until root intrusion or accumulated debris backs the line up entirely.

How we clear a drain

Most kitchen, bathroom, and single-fixture clogs clear with a drain snake, a flexible cable run down the line to break up or retrieve the blockage. For grease-heavy kitchen lines or a drain that clogs repeatedly, hydro jetting does a more thorough job: high-pressure water scours the full interior of the pipe rather than just punching through the clog.

Mainline and sewer lateral clogs

When more than one drain is slow or several fixtures back up at once, the problem is rarely any single drain — it is the mainline or sewer lateral leaving the house. We run a sewer camera before recommending a fix on mainline calls, because a mainline clog and a mainline leak look identical from inside the house but need completely different repairs. If the camera shows a genuine leak, see our sewer line leak page instead.

If instead you are seeing water where it should not be — a wet patch, a warm floor, a climbing bill — that points toward a leak rather than a clog.

Drain cleaning questions from La Puente homeowners

Snaking or hydro jetting: which do I need?

Snaking is faster and cheaper for a single stubborn clog. Jetting costs more but scours the full pipe wall and lasts longer, worth it for grease-heavy kitchen lines or a drain that clogs repeatedly.

Multiple drains are slow at once. Is that one problem?

Almost always — the mainline leaving the house rather than each fixture individually, which calls for a camera inspection instead of snaking each drain separately.

Do you offer commercial drain cleaning?

Yes, for restaurants, retail, and other commercial spaces across La Puente, West Covina, and the San Gabriel Valley.
