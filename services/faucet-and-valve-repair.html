Hard water wears out moving parts, not just pipe

Main San Gabriel Basin groundwater runs roughly 10 to 17 grains per gallon here, and the same mineral load that pits copper pipe from the inside wears down faucet cartridges, washers, and valve seats faster than manufacturers' national estimates assume. A cartridge rated for a decade of use often starts dripping in five or six years in La Puente. Shutoff valves fare worse: many 1950s and 1960s tract homes still carry original gate valves, and gate valves seize with mineral buildup far more readily than the ball valves used in newer construction.

A shutoff valve that will not turn is not a minor inconvenience. It means you cannot isolate that fixture in an emergency, which turns a small drip into a bigger problem the day something actually fails.

What we fix under this service

Dripping faucets. Almost always a worn cartridge, O-ring, or washer, and we stock parts for the common builder-grade brands found across La Puente's tract housing. Shower and tub valves. A valve that will not hold temperature or has become stiff to turn usually needs a cartridge or stem replacement. Shutoff and supply valves. Seized main shutoffs and stuck angle stops under sinks — if your main valve will not turn freely, we replace it with a quarter-turn ball valve so it actually works in an emergency. Outdoor hose bibs that drip or won't shut fully, and burst or broken supply lines, the flexible connector between a shutoff valve and a fixture.

Why we test the shutoff before touching the fixture

Before repairing any faucet or valve, we confirm the local shutoff actually isolates it. On a meaningful share of La Puente calls, the isolation valve is as seized as the fixture itself, turning what looked like a five-minute cartridge swap into a two-part repair. Faucet & valve questions from La Puente homeowners

My faucet only drips at night. Is that normal?

It is more noticeable when the house is quiet, but the cause is the same worn cartridge or washer regardless of when you notice it, and La Puente's mineral content tends to accelerate the wear once it starts.

Why won't my main shutoff valve turn?

Original gate valves in homes built through the 1960s seize with age and mineral buildup. We can sometimes free one carefully, but if it has been stuck for years, replacing it with a ball valve is the safer fix.

Is a slow dripping outdoor faucet worth fixing?

A slow drip outside can waste thousands of gallons a year and may signal the start of a crack. Worth a same-visit fix rather than letting it run.
